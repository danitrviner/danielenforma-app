# Respuesta a Apple — Guideline 1.4.1 (Submission 48cf75e9-0a73-4f93-880e-b94998ee85e7)

Pegar en App Store Connect > Resolution Center al subir el build nuevo.

---

Hello,

Thank you for the review. We have added citations for all health and nutrition
information shown in the app, and made them easy to find.

**What we added in this build:**

1. **A dedicated "Fuentes científicas" (Scientific sources) screen.** It lists,
   grouped by topic, every calculation and reference value the app shows the
   user, together with the full bibliographic citation and a tappable link to
   the original source (PubMed, EFSA, WHO, FAO, ACSM, USDA FoodData Central and
   the Spanish food composition database BEDCA).

2. **Access from the nutrition section itself**, which is where the issue was
   reported. The link "Fuentes científicas y aviso médico" is at the bottom of
   the Nutrition screen, present on every nutrition tab (Mi Menú, Mi Plan,
   Recetas, Periodización).

3. **Inline citations in the same screens as the numbers.** Below the exchange
   plan and below the periodization panel there is a note naming the sources of
   each calculation (Mifflin-St Jeor 1990, Hall 2008, EFSA Dietary Reference
   Values, WHO sodium guideline), with a "Ver fuentes" link that opens the full
   list. The micronutrient panel carries the same note.

4. **The same access from the Cardio screen** (estimated maximum heart rate and
   training zones — Tanaka et al. 2001, ACSM Guidelines) and from
   Profile > Settings > Legal.

5. **A prominent medical disclaimer** at the top of that screen: the app is a
   coaching support tool, not a medical device, the information is educational
   and general, it does not replace a physician or a registered dietitian, and
   users are told to consult a professional before starting a diet or exercise
   programme.

**Where to find it (review steps):**
Sign in with the demo account (atleta@enforma.com) > tab "Nutrición" > scroll to
the bottom of the screen > tap "Fuentes científicas y aviso médico". The same
link is also at the bottom of the "Cardio" tab and in
Perfil > Ajustes > Legal > "Fuentes científicas y aviso médico".

Please let us know if you need anything else.
